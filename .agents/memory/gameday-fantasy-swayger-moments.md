description: Durable identity and immutability rules for named Weekly Fantasy questions.
---

Weekly Fantasy Moment titles and objective definitions derive from the stable
template ID. The source library may receive clearer future-publish prompts and
new non-default templates, but already-published weekly props must not be
rewritten.

**Why:** The live pilot Week 2 had saved picks when named Moments were added.
Deriving the title preserved its historical prompt, answers, points, and
settlement meaning while allowing the new presentation layer to appear.

**How to apply:** Add future Moment presentation through the stable-ID registry.
Change source-library rows only for future publications. Never backfill
published prop questions or answer snapshots for a naming/copy upgrade.
