---
name: Impact commercial review boundaries
description: Safety rules for reviewing affiliate offers before any future publishing workflow
---

Commercial Review is an internal, read-only inspection step. “Select for Swayger” or “Shortlist” must not publish, assign, persist, or feed offers into Fantasy Weekly or Game Day.

**Why:** Impact inventory contains partner-supplied offers and creatives, while approval and placement decisions belong to a later product workflow. Treating availability as approval would create an unsafe commercial boundary.

**How to apply:** Keep the review endpoint separate from the full inventory endpoint, use normalized data only, label ads as “available to partner,” and require a separately designed approval/persistence flow before adding any write action.
