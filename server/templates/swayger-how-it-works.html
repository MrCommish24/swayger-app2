How it works

Make your picks. Prove your takes. Keep the receipts.

The competitive prediction layer for sports communities.

Three ways to turn the conversation into competition. Bring your group, join a room, or challenge a friend.

Pick. Lock. Compete. Settle. Share.

  1. Pick. Choose your answers in a Game Day room, a Fantasy competition, or a 1v1 challenge.
  2. Lock. Confirm your picks before the competition's pick window closes. Timing and edit rules depend on the format.
  3. Compete. Follow the standings as results are confirmed.
  4. Settle. Hosts and commissioners confirm results using the competition's rules. In 1v1, participants use the challenge's result-confirmation flow.
  5. Share. Keep a receipt of the final outcome and share it with your group. Shared links still follow the competition's access rules.
01 / GAME DAY

One room. Everyone's takes.

Open a host's invite link or explore available public rooms. Supported rooms let you join as a guest without an account. Make your picks within the room's pick window and track the leaderboard as results are confirmed.

Formats have different rules. A single-game competition and a Madden Pick Card do not necessarily score the same way. Check the room's rules; main picks, Bonus Game, and exact Total Points can be tracked separately.

02 / FANTASY

Your league. Another reason to compete.

A commissioner creates the league and adds its teams. Members open the invite link and claim their team with an account or as a guest. Participate in Draft Day and weekly prediction competitions, then return for results and receipts.

Commissioners manage publishing, pick windows, and result confirmation. Guest access is tied to your device unless you connect an account; keep your invite or recovery link.

03 / 1v1

One friend. Two takes. One receipt.

  1. Create the challenge. Sign in, open Swaygers, and choose your prediction and challenge terms.
  2. Invite your opponent. Share the challenge's link or code. They can preview an invite before signing in to participate.
  3. Confirm and compete. Your opponent adds their pick and accepts the challenge. Follow the existing result-confirmation flow when the outcome is known.
  4. Keep the receipt. Share the confirmed outcome and let the record do the talking.

Run the competition. Let Swayger handle the rest.

Already have the group? Bring Swayger to it. Group chats, Discord communities, fantasy leagues, and people watching together can use the same three competition modes. NFL, Madden, and in-venue play are examples—not separate products.
