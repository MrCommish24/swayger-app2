We are building a focused fast-follow:

# SWAYGER FANTASY WEEKLY RECEIPT V1
## Finalized Week Results + Shareable Image + Short Link + Week 2 Re-engagement

This is NOT a new product phase.

Week 1 of Swayger Fantasy Weekly has now been completed in a real pilot league.

Current state:

- Weekly Results screen exists and looks good
- Personal Weekly Results show:
  - winner/co-winners
  - standings
  - My Picks
  - correct answers
- League Picks exists separately
- Draft Day has a separate Global Draft Day Receipt
- Draft Day also has:
  - compact share image
  - short receipt alias
  - share/copy controls
- Weekly currently does NOT have an equivalent shareable global receipt

The current workaround is:
- manually screenshot Weekly Results
- paste screenshot into group chat
- manually type commentary

We want the product to do that.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT GOAL
==================================================

Create a finalized-only:

# WEEKLY GLOBAL RECEIPT

for Swayger Fantasy.

It should answer:

"What happened in our fantasy league this week?"

not:

"How did I personally do?"

It should support:

- league-level Week N results
- winner/co-winners
- compact standings
- final correct answers
- shareable branded image
- short link
- concise auto-generated share text
- re-engagement into the next week

==================================================
2. PRESERVE EXISTING THREE-LAYER MODEL
==================================================

We want three distinct experiences:

1. PERSONAL WEEKLY RESULTS
   - existing
   - viewer-specific
   - includes My Picks / Your Pick
   - keep unchanged

2. WEEKLY GLOBAL RECEIPT
   - NEW
   - league-level
   - identical for all authorized league members
   - winner + standings + weekly outcomes
   - designed for sharing

3. LEAGUE PICKS
   - existing
   - who picked what
   - detailed social receipts
   - keep separate

Do NOT merge all three.
