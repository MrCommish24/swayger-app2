# NFL Game Day V1

The objective is to make Swayger Game Day genuinely football-ready for the NFL season without breaking the existing NBA/World Cup-style Game Day experience.

# 6. NFL prop design principles

All V1 props must be:

* objective
* easy to understand
* quickly answerable
* easy for the host to settle
* relevant to the phase
* not dependent on subjective judgment
* not wagering language
* appropriate as a social sports-prediction competition

Do not include subjective prompts such as:

* biggest play
* most clutch player
* best drive
* most impressive performance
* momentum winner
* who "looks better"

Avoid props requiring obscure stat interpretation.

Prefer binary, team, player, threshold, range, or multiple-choice outcomes.

# 8. Pregame prop library

Build a strong initial pregame library.

Include objective options such as:

### Winner / game state

* Who wins the game?
* Who leads at halftime?
* Which team scores first?
* Which team scores the first touchdown?
* Will the game be tied at any point after 0–0?
* Which team scores last?

### First-score / scoring

* First score type:

  * touchdown
  * field goal
  * other/safety where appropriate
* Will both teams score a touchdown in the first half?
* Will either team score 3+ touchdowns?

### QB

* Which QB throws more touchdown passes?
* Which QB has more passing yards?
* Will `{TEAM_A_QB}` throw 2+ touchdown passes?
* Will `{TEAM_B_QB}` throw 2+ touchdown passes?
* Will either starting QB throw an interception?

### Rushing / receiving

* Will `{TEAM_A_STAR}` score a touchdown?
* Will `{TEAM_B_STAR}` score a touchdown?
* Will `{TEAM_A_STAR}` reach an appropriate yardage threshold?
* Will `{TEAM_B_STAR}` reach an appropriate yardage threshold?

Use thresholds that are configurable/template-driven rather than pretending one fixed threshold fits every player.

### Turnovers / special teams

* Will both teams commit a turnover?
* Will there be 3+ total turnovers?
* Will either team miss a field goal?
* Will there be a field goal of 50+ yards?

### Game totals/ranges

Avoid sportsbook-style over/under presentation.

Use social prediction wording such as:

> How many total touchdowns will be scored?

Options:

* 0–3
* 4–5
* 6+

or another sensible range.

Also consider:

* winning margin range
* total touchdown range
* first-half touchdown range

Do not build odds, spreads, payouts, or gambling UI.

# 10. Halftime prop strategy

Halftime questions must reflect that the first half has already happened.

Do not simply repeat Pregame props.

They should predict the **second half** or final result.

Build an initial Halftime library including:

* Who wins the game?
* Which team scores first in the second half?
* Which team scores more points in the second half?
* Will the team currently trailing come back to win?
* Which QB throws more second-half TD passes?
* Will either QB throw a second-half interception?
* Will `{TEAM_A_STAR}` score in the second half?
* Will `{TEAM_B_STAR}` score in the second half?
* Will there be 3+ second-half touchdowns?
* Will there be a fourth-quarter lead change?
* Winning margin range

Avoid questions whose answer is already known at halftime.

# 12. 4Q / Clutch prop strategy

Build a Late Game library such as:

Avoid props that require a full extra quarter of detailed stat tracking.
