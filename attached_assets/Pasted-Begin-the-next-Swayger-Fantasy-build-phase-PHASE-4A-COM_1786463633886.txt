V1 DRAFT DAY STRUCTURE

Use ONE primary Draft Day phase:

phase = 'draft_day'

Do NOT require:
- pre_draft
- in_draft
- post_draft

Those values remain available in schema for future experimentation only.

Within the single Draft Day experience, visually distinguish two types of questions:

1. DRAFT DAY PICKS
- scoring_scope = 'competition'
- resolve during or at the end of the fantasy draft
- count toward the Draft Day winner

2. SEASON RECEIPTS
- scoring_scope = 'season'
- lock on Draft Day
- remain pending for weeks/months
- do NOT count toward the Draft Day winner
- resolve later during the fantasy season

Example:

DRAFT DAY PICKS
- Who drafts a QB first?
- Who makes the first reach?
- Who drafts the first rookie?
- Who takes the longest on the clock?

SEASON RECEIPTS
- Who finishes first in the league?
- Who scores the most points this season?
- Who has the best draft?
- Who finishes last?

Do not treat “Who has the best draft?” as mandatory if its settlement definition is unclear. Use only questions that can be meaningfully settled.

DUPLICATE DRAFT DAY SAFETY

A league season should not accidentally get two competing V1 Draft Day competitions from double taps/retries.

Do not necessarily add a schema migration unless needed.

At minimum:
- UI disables Publish while request pending
- server/RPC checks for existing Draft Day competition for league_season_id
- retry is idempotent or returns the existing competition
- no duplicate room + props from double submit

Remember: multiple drafts may eventually exist in dynasty/startup formats.

Therefore do NOT add a permanent schema rule that fantasy seasons can only ever have one Draft Day room.

V1 behavior can treat one “primary Draft Day Swayger” as the setup default without making that a foundational DB restriction.
