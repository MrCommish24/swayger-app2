==================================================
1. PRODUCT GOAL
==================================================

A fantasy commissioner may have a default weekly reward but occasionally want a different reward for a particular week.

Examples:

Season default:
$25

Week 1:
$25

Week 2:
Dinner at Buffalo Wild Wings

Week 3:
No reward

Week 4:
$25

We need to support this without creating a prize-management or payout system.

The model should remain:

# SEASON DEFAULT
+
# OPTIONAL WEEK-SPECIFIC OVERRIDE

8. SNAPSHOT PRINCIPLE — CRITICAL
==================================================

At PUBLISH time, determine the effective Week N reward and store it on:

gameday_rooms.reward_description
gameday_rooms.reward_amount_display

Therefore:

Season default today = $25

Publish Week 1:
Week 1 room snapshots $25

Later commissioner changes season default to $50.

Expected:

Week 1 remains $25

Future Week 2 using season default:
snapshots $50

Historical rewards must NOT depend on the current season default.
