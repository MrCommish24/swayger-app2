==================================================
4. EVENT DEFINITIONS
==================================================

Be precise.

Examples:

`fantasy_week_viewed`
fires when an authorized member successfully opens a Week Swayger play/results route.

Do NOT fire on failed/unauthorized route attempts.

`fantasy_week_pick_started`
fires once per competition per participant when the first competition-scope pick is saved.

Do not fire repeatedly for every edit.

`fantasy_week_pick_submitted`
may fire on pick save if useful, but do not confuse this with completion.

`fantasy_week_pick_completed`
fires when the participant has answered all competition-scope Week props for the current published card.

This should be idempotent per participant/week where practical.

`fantasy_week_locked`
fires when commissioner successfully locks the Week.

`fantasy_week_finalized`
fires only after successful finalization.

`fantasy_league_picks_viewed`
fires only when League Picks are actually revealed/viewable after lock.

`fantasy_receipt_viewed`
fires when the finalized Global Receipt successfully loads.

`fantasy_receipt_shared`
fires when Share action is initiated successfully according to existing platform conventions.

`fantasy_receipt_link_copied`
fires when Copy Receipt Link succeeds.
