PART 2 — REVISE LATE MEMBER PRODUCT RULE
--------------------------------------------------

We are changing the proposed V1 behavior.

CURRENT RULE:

Once global pick_count > 0:
→ new member is draft_day_eligible=false
→ Add to League Only

NEW PROPOSED RULE:

The Draft Day LOCK should be the primary roster freeze.

If:

card.status = 'open'

the commissioner may add a new member even if picks already exist.

New member should:

- join league
- join current season
- be draft_day_eligible=true
- be able to claim their seat
- be able to make Draft Day picks
- be added to relevant published answer_options

Existing picks should NOT be deleted.

Existing players remain free to revise their picks until card locks.

If:

card.status = 'locked'

then:
→ member may be added to persistent league/season
→ draft_day_eligible=false for THIS Draft Day
→ current answer_options unchanged
→ existing Draft Day remains frozen
