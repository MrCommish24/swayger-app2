The Viral Mechanic: Receipts

A Receipt is a simple, shareable visual proof of a settled Swayger.

Think of it like a scorecard or bet slip, but social.

Example:

SWAYGER RECEIPT

Darius vs Mike

Who wins Game 7?
Darius: Celtics
Mike: Mavericks

Result: Darius Wins
+5 Units

Record vs Mike: 3–1

Then a Share button exports it as an image.

Users post it to:

Instagram

Twitter/X

group chats

Discord

text threads

Every share naturally includes:

“Settle it with friends on Swayger.”

That’s organic growth.

Why this works

Social wagers are about being right, not money.

People want to say:

“I told you so.”

But right now your app keeps that inside the app.

Receipts let users broadcast wins outside the app.

Not immediately.

MVP version (very easy technically)

You don't need image generation servers.

React Native can render a receipt card component and export it as an image.

Libraries:

react-native-view-shot
expo-sharing

Flow:

Settlement confirmed
↓
Show “Share Receipt”
↓
Generate image
↓
Open native share sheet
Replit prompt for when you're ready
Add a "Swayger Receipt" share feature for settled wagers.

When a Swayger reaches status = settled:

1. Show a "Share Receipt" button on the Swayger detail screen.

2. Create a receipt card component that displays:
   - Swayger title
   - creator username
   - opponent username
   - creator pick
   - opponent pick
   - outcome
   - units won
   - settled date

3. Render the receipt as a visually styled card.

4. Use react-native-view-shot to capture the card as an image.

5. Use expo-sharing to open the native share sheet so users can share to social apps.

6. Include small footer branding:
   "Settled on Swayger"

Do not add backend logic. This should be purely a UI share layer.
