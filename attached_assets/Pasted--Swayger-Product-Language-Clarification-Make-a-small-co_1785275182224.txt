# Swayger Product-Language Clarification

Make a small copy-only improvement to the Game Day home screen so users can immediately understand the difference between:

- Game Day Rooms: group competitions around live games
- My Swaygers: 1v1 sports challenges

Do not add onboarding screens, modals, tutorials, tooltips, or new navigation.

Do not change functionality, layout structure, data fetching, routing, authentication, analytics, or styling beyond minor spacing required for the new text.

## File scope

Expected file:

`app/(tabs)/index.tsx`

Do not modify any other file unless absolutely necessary. If another file is required, stop and explain why before making changes.

## Current screen

When no Game Day Room is live, the Game Day home currently shows:

Headline:
“Nothing live right now”

Body:
“Game Day Rooms appear here before and during featured games.”

Invite guidance:
“Have an invite link? Open it from your text or group chat to join directly.”

The My Swaygers card currently shows:

“My Swaygers”

For an existing user:

- Active Swayger count
- Swayger Points balance
- “View My Swaygers →”

For a zero-Swayger user:

- “Challenge a friend to a 1v1 sports prediction.”
- “View My Swaygers →”

## Objective

Clarify the product architecture directly on this screen:

- Game Day Rooms are the group experience.
- My Swaygers are the 1v1 experience.
- Both are experiences within Swayger.

This should be accomplished through concise descriptors, not through additional onboarding.

## Change 1: Clarify Game Day Rooms

Replace:

“Game Day Rooms appear here before and during featured games.”

With:

“Game Day Rooms are group pick’em competitions that open before and during featured games.”

Keep the invite guidance directly below it:

“Have an invite link? Open it from your text or group chat to join directly.”

Do not change how invite links work. Do not add an invite-code or URL-entry field.

## Change 2: Clarify My Swaygers for existing users

For authenticated users with one or more Swaygers, add this short descriptor directly beneath the “My Swaygers” card title:

“Your 1v1 sports challenges”

Then retain the existing dynamic statistics:

- Active count
- Swayger Points balance

Retain the existing CTA:

“View My Swaygers →”

The intended structure is:

My Swaygers

Your 1v1 sports challenges

3 Active | 1,671 SP Balance

View My Swaygers →

Use the existing secondary-text style where possible. The descriptor should be visually secondary to the card title and should not compete with the statistics.

## Change 3: Preserve the zero-Swayger version

For an authenticated user with zero Swaygers, retain the existing educational copy:

“Challenge a friend to a 1v1 sports prediction.”

Do not also show “Your 1v1 sports challenges” in this state. Avoid redundant wording.

The zero-Swayger structure should remain:

My Swaygers

Challenge a friend to a 1v1 sports prediction.

View My Swaygers →

## Change 4: Preserve loading and unauthenticated behavior

Do not treat loading as a confirmed zero-Swayger state.

Preserve the existing logic that distinguishes:

- Query loading
- Confirmed empty Swayger array
- Existing Swayger activity
- Unauthenticated user

Do not introduce a flash of zero-state copy while the Swaygers query is still loading.

## Live-room behavior

Do not change the live-room experience.

When a public room is live:

- Keep the featured room hero unchanged
- Keep the “LIVE NOW” badge unchanged
- Keep “Join & Make Picks →” unchanged
- Keep additional-room behavior unchanged
- Do not insert educational copy that competes with the live-room CTA

This prompt is focused on the no-live-room screen and the My Swaygers fallback card.

## Terminology standards

Use these names consistently:

- Overall product: `Swayger`
- Group live-game experience: `Game Day`
- Individual group competition: `Game Day Room`
- 1v1 experience: `My Swaygers`
- Individual head-to-head experience: `1v1 Swayger`

Do not use:

- Game Day Swayger
- Gameday Swayger
- Swayger Room
- Swayger Board

Use `Game Day` as two words.
