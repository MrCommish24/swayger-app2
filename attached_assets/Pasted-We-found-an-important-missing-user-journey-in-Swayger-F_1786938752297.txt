We found an important missing user journey in Swayger Fantasy Season.

# PHASE 5.1 — FANTASY SEASON RE-ENGAGEMENT

Current state:

- Draft Day works
- Week 1 Swayger works
- existing authenticated members return without reclaiming seats
- existing guest claims persist on the same browser/device
- Week 1 play, settlement, results, and Season Standings exist
- Phase 5 test suite is green

The missing piece is:

HOW DOES THE COMMISSIONER GET PEOPLE TO COME BACK?

Scenario:

A member participates in Draft Day.

Draft Day ends.

They do not win.

They forget about Swayger.

A few weeks/days later the commissioner creates Week 1 and says:

“Don’t forget, we’re doing weekly Swaygers.”

Right now Swayger handles the member correctly once they return, but we have not built the re-engagement loop that gets them back.

This phase should solve that.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

DO NOT treat Week 1 as a new invitation.

The person is already a member of the Swayger Fantasy league.

The mental model should be:

DRAFT DAY:
“Join our Swayger Fantasy league.”

WEEK 1:
“Week 1 Swayger is live. Make your picks.”

WEEK 2:
“Week 2 Swayger is live. Make your picks.”

etc.

Do not make users rejoin, reclaim a seat, or repeat onboarding every week.

==================================================
2. V1 RE-ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY
==================================================

Do NOT build automated email, SMS, push notifications, Twilio, or scheduled reminders yet.

For V1, the commissioner manually shares a direct Week Swayger link into the communication channel the league already uses:

- iMessage
- SMS
- WhatsApp
- Discord
- GroupMe
- Slack
- Facebook Messenger
- email
- etc.

Swayger should make this extremely easy.

The core feature is:

[ 📣 Share Week 1 ]

==================================================
3. SHARE WEEK 1 CTA
==================================================

After Week 1 is published, add a clear commissioner CTA on the WeeklyCard:

📣 Share Week 1

This should exist while Week 1 is open.

Do not call it:

Invite Members

because these users already belong to the league.

Use language such as:

Share Week 1

or

Share Week 1 Swayger

==================================================
4. DIRECT WEEK 1 LINK
==================================================

The shared link should send users as directly as possible to Week 1.

Preferred destination:

Week 1 play screen for the existing Fantasy league/season/week.

Use the existing route architecture.

The shared URL must include enough information to identify:

- league
- season
- week

Do NOT encode member identity into the URL.

Identity continues to resolve through:

- authenticated user claim
- existing guest token

Do not create a new weekly invitation token.

==================================================
5. RETURNING AUTHENTICATED MEMBER JOURNEY
==================================================

Expected:

Commissioner shares Week 1 link.

Existing authenticated member taps link.

Swayger resolves their existing Fantasy membership.

They land directly at:

WEEK 1 SWAYGER
Make Your Picks

No:

- join screen
- seat selection
- team selection
- create league
- re-invite
- “Who are you?” prompt

If they are signed out but previously had an authenticated claim:

follow existing auth behavior.

Do not invent a new identity system in this phase.

==================================================
6. RETURNING GUEST JOURNEY
==================================================

Existing guest on the SAME browser/device:

Commissioner shares Week 1 link.

Guest opens it.

Existing durable Fantasy guest token resolves their claim.

They go directly to Week 1.

No new guest token.

No new seat claim.

No duplicate league member.

==================================================
7. GUEST CROSS-DEVICE EDGE CASE
==================================================

Inspect current behavior for:

Guest joined Draft Day on Device A
→ opens Week 1 link on Device B

Do NOT overbuild recovery in this phase.

But return exact current behavior.

If the guest cannot be recognized on Device B:

- do not silently create another league member
- do not duplicate their seat
- show a safe recovery path if one already exists

If no safe recovery flow exists, report this clearly as a known limitation.

Do not build account linking/recovery unless required to prevent duplicate identity.

- at least one eligible member has not played

[LINK]

The commissioner can see who is missing privately.

14. DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY SAY “LOCKS SOON”
==================================================

Current V1 uses manual commissioner locking.

Therefore we may not have a real lock deadline.

Do not claim:

“Picks lock tonight at 7 PM”

unless a real lock time exists.

Safer generic copy:

“Make your picks before they lock.”

For reminder:

“Still time to make your Week 1 picks.”

If the product has a real configured deadline field, inspect and use it.

Do not invent one.

==================================================
19. WEEK LINK ROUTING
==================================================

Test all relevant entry conditions.

A. Authenticated existing member:
shared link → Week 1 play

B. Existing guest on same device:
shared link → Week 1 play

C. Commissioner:
shared link → Week 1 play as their own member identity

D. User is valid league member but Week is locked:
link → read-only Week 1 picks

E. Week finalized:
link → Week 1 Results, or clear finalized state with View Results CTA

Do not land someone on a dead-end “cannot play” page if a better destination exists.

==================================================
20. NON-MEMBER OPENS WEEK LINK
==================================================

This needs deliberate behavior.

Scenario:

someone forwards the Week 1 link to a person who is NOT already in the Fantasy league.

Do not automatically create a new league member.

Preferred:

show the existing Fantasy join/claim experience for that league/season IF the league still allows joining.

But be careful:

if Week 1 is already locked, a new member may join the persistent league but should not become eligible for the current week.

Reuse existing open-roster rules.

Do not invent a separate weekly invitation flow.

==================================================
21. DIRECT LINK SECURITY
==================================================

The link itself is not authorization.

Server must continue verifying:

- league/season
- valid Fantasy membership/claim
- current weekly eligibility

Do not expose protected weekly data just because someone knows the URL.

==================================================
23. PLAYED DEFINITION TEST
==================================================

Critical:

League has 10 eligible members.

- 2 never open Week 1
- 2 open Week 1 but make no picks
- 6 save at least one pick

Expected:

played_count = 6
waiting_count = 4
eligible_count = 10

Opening the screen alone does not count.

==================================================
24. OPEN-ROSTER INTERACTION
==================================================

If commissioner adds a new member while Week 1 is open:

eligible_count should update.

Example:

6/10 played
→ new member joins
→ 6/11 played

If they make a pick:
→ 7/11

If new member is added after lock:
they are NOT eligible for current Week 1 and should NOT increase Week 1 eligible_count.

Use current `draft_day_eligible` / weekly eligibility behavior correctly.

If the existing field name is Draft-Day-specific and makes this awkward, inspect before changing schema.

Do not create SQL unless necessary.
