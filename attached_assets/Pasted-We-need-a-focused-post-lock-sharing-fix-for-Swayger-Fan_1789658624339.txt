We need a focused post-lock sharing fix for Swayger Fantasy Weekly.

# SWAYGER RUN V1B.1 — KEEP PICKS SHAREABLE AFTER LOCK

Context:

Swayger Run V1B is implemented and working well.

Manual QA confirmed the current locked-week behavior:

- participant picks become read-only
- My Lock becomes read-only
- League Picks becomes visible
- commissioner can resolve the week

However, current behavior also removes:

- Share Pick
- Share My Lock

We want to change that.

Product rule:

LOCKED = IMMUTABLE, NOT UNSHAREABLE.

A locked prediction is actually more valuable socially because the participant can no longer change it.

Desired lifecycle:

BEFORE LOCK
- picks editable
- Share Pick available
- My Lock selectable/changeable
- Share My Lock available

AFTER LOCK
- picks read-only
- My Lock read-only
- Share Pick remains available
- Share My Lock remains available
- League Picks follows existing reveal behavior
- no prediction or My Lock mutation allowed

AFTER SETTLEMENT / FINALIZATION
- picks remain read-only
- My Lock remains read-only
- sharing remains available unless an existing lifecycle/security rule makes this unsafe
- do NOT add result-specific receipt logic in this task

This is a focused lifecycle/share-access change only.

2. LIFECYCLE RULE
==================================================

Implement this rule explicitly:

EDITABILITY and SHAREABILITY are separate concerns.

EDITABILITY:

OPEN:
- picks editable
- My Lock editable

LOCKED:
- picks read-only
- My Lock read-only

SETTLED / FINALIZED:
- picks read-only
- My Lock read-only

SHAREABILITY:

OPEN:
- confirmed picks shareable
- My Lock shareable

LOCKED:
- confirmed picks shareable
- My Lock shareable

SETTLED / FINALIZED:
- confirmed picks shareable
- My Lock shareable unless an existing security or route constraint makes this unsafe

Do not infer shareability from editability.

5. NO NEW MY LOCK AFTER LOCK
==================================================

If the participant did not choose My Lock before lock:

Do NOT allow them to create one after lock.

Reason:

My Lock is meant to represent confidence declared before predictions freeze.

Post-lock behavior:

- no Choose My Lock
- no Call Your Shot selection
- no lock mutation

They may still share any existing confirmed individual pick using Share Pick.
