# Swayger Fantasy — Pre-Build Product & Architecture Assessment

Do not write code, apply migrations, or modify the application yet.

We need a full pre-build assessment for **Swayger Fantasy**, beginning with **Draft Day** and intentionally designed to support a **season-long weekly competition experience immediately afterward**.

The objective is to avoid building Draft Day as a standalone feature that we then have to restructure for Week 1.

The guiding principle is:

**Design the season. Build Draft Day first.**

---

# 1. Product objective

Swayger Fantasy is not intended to replace ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, NFL Fantasy, or another fantasy platform.

Those platforms continue to manage:

* fantasy rosters
* drafts
* lineups
* trades
* waivers
* player scoring
* matchup results
* league standings

Swayger sits on top of the existing fantasy league as an **engagement and prediction layer**.

The core value proposition is:

**Who knows their fantasy league best?**

Swayger Fantasy should allow league members to make predictions about:

* their draft
* their league members
* their fantasy teams
* weekly league results
* fantasy players
* season outcomes

Those predictions create:

* weekly competition
* bragging rights
* receipts
* weekly winners
* season-long Swayger standings
* optional commissioner-defined rewards

---

# 2. Intended user journey

The intended long-term journey is:

Fantasy League Setup
→ Draft Day Swayger
→ Week 1 Swayger
→ Week 2
→ Week 3
→ continuing weekly competitions
→ Fantasy Playoffs
→ Championship
→ Season Swayger Champion

Draft Day is the initial onboarding event.

A commissioner may tell the league at Draft Day:

**“This season we are also doing a weekly Swayger competition, and each week's Swayger winner receives a prize.”**

Therefore, league members should enter the Swayger ecosystem at Draft Day and remain connected to that same fantasy league for the entire season.

We do NOT want:

* members rejoining every week
* commissioners re-entering league members every week
* unrelated weekly rooms
* duplicate league identities
* Draft Day scoring that cannot carry into season standings
* Draft Day data models that must immediately be replaced

# 4. Define the Swayger Fantasy domain model

Before proposing implementation, define the major entities and relationships.

At minimum, assess these concepts.

## Fantasy League

A persistent fantasy community.

Examples:

* Sunday Smoke
* 2026 season
* football
* 12 managers

It should persist across Draft Day, Week 1, Week 2, playoffs, etc.

## Fantasy League Member

A person who belongs to that fantasy league for the season.

Membership must persist independently from participation in any individual weekly competition.

## Fantasy Team

The fantasy roster/team controlled by a league member.

Examples:

Member:
Darius

Fantasy Team:
The Monstars

These may need to be separate concepts.

Assess whether the application should model:

* person/member identity
* fantasy team identity
* relationship between member and team

Do not assume a fantasy team name and member display name are the same thing.

## Commissioner

The person responsible for managing the fantasy league's Swayger experience.

## League Admin / Co-Commissioner

Consider whether the data model should support more than one league administrator in the future even if the initial UI only supports one commissioner.

## Competition / Event

An individual Swayger competition inside the Fantasy League.

Examples:

* Draft Day
* Week 1
* Week 2
* Week 10
* Playoffs
* Championship

## Competition Participant

A league member who participates in a specific competition.

League membership and competition participation are different concepts.

A league member might miss Week 7 and participate again in Week 8.

## Pick Card / Phase

A group of props with a common open/lock cadence.

## Prop

A prediction question.

## Pick

A member's prediction.

## Competition Result

Winner and standings for an individual competition.

## Season Swayger Standing

Cumulative season performance across multiple Fantasy competitions.

Recommend the minimum clean schema that supports these concepts.

# 5. Reassess whether we need persistent Fantasy League tables now

The previous Draft Day assessment recommended postponing a `fantasy_leagues` table.

That assumption has changed.

Season-long Fantasy is a planned immediate fast follow.

Compare:

### Option A — Room-as-League

Draft Day room itself stores league identity.

### Option B — Persistent Fantasy League

Create persistent structures such as:

* `fantasy_leagues`
* `fantasy_league_members`
* possibly `fantasy_teams`

Then existing Game Day rooms or another reusable event object become competitions belonging to that league.

Assess both approaches.

Recommend the architecture that avoids meaningful rework when Week 1 is implemented.

Do not optimize only for the smallest Draft Day migration.

Assess whether a current `gameday_room` can represent an individual Fantasy competition.

Conceptually:

Each competition may continue using:

If viable, recommend the minimum relationship needed between:

If `gameday_rooms` is too tightly tied to live sports matchups, explain why and recommend the minimum safe abstraction.

Do NOT create parallel Fantasy prop, pick, settlement, leaderboard, or scoring systems unless the existing infrastructure genuinely cannot support the requirement.

# 7. Make the architecture sports agnostic

This is a hard requirement.

Swayger Fantasy may initially launch for football, but the underlying architecture should support:

* fantasy football
* fantasy basketball
* fantasy baseball
* other fantasy sports

Sport-specific prop templates are acceptable.

NFL-specific core data relationships are not.

Reassess the prior recommendation to use:

`sport = 'fantasy_draft'`

Fantasy Draft is not a sport.

Assess whether we should separate concepts such as:

* sport
* experience type
* competition type

For example only:

* sport = football
* experience_type = fantasy
* competition_type = draft_day

and later:

* sport = football
* experience_type = fantasy
* competition_type = weekly

Do not blindly implement these exact values.

Recommend the cleanest taxonomy based on the current application.

# 9. League member onboarding and identity claiming

At Draft Day, the commissioner may already know the league members and team names.

Potential experience:

* Darius — The Monstars

* Anthony — Gridiron Kings

**Who are you?**

Swayger then associates:

* authenticated account
  or
* guest/device/session

with that persistent league-member record.

Assess this approach versus allowing every user to enter a new display name.

Specifically address:

* duplicate names
* spelling differences
* Mike versus Mike T
* guest sessions
* authenticated users
* someone later converting from guest to authenticated
* member replacement
* team ownership transfer
* co-managers
* inactive league members
* commissioner resolving identity conflicts

Recommend the simplest reliable approach for Draft Day that also supports Week 1 without re-onboarding.

# 11. Answer-option architecture

# 13. Season-end props created on Draft Day

This is a critical requirement.

We want Draft Day to include predictions that will not resolve until much later.

Examples:

* Who wins the fantasy championship?
* Who finishes first in regular-season scoring?
* Who finishes last?
* Which team earns the #1 playoff seed?
* Which manager misses the playoffs?

These props should:

* be created during Draft Day
* be locked during Draft Day
* remain visible as long-term receipts
* remain unresolved for weeks or months
* NOT prevent Draft Day itself from being finalized
* NOT prevent a Draft Day winner from being declared
* eventually contribute to season-long Fantasy standings when settled

Assess how the current room/card/prop/finalization architecture handles unresolved props.

If current finalization assumes all props must settle, identify the minimum architectural change required.

We need a clean concept for a prop's **resolution horizon** or scoring scope.

Examples of possible concepts:

* draft_event
* weekly
* season_long

Do not implement those exact values unless appropriate.

Recommend the cleanest model.

# 14. Scoring architecture

We need to separate the following concepts:

## Competition score

Points earned in one event.

Example:

Week 3 Swayger.

This determines the weekly winner.

## Fantasy season score

Cumulative Fantasy performance across:

* Draft Day
* weekly competitions
* season-long props
* playoffs

This determines the Season Swayger Champion.

## Broader Swayger Points

If the application currently uses SP as a broader user-level value, assess whether Fantasy scoring should be directly tied to that system.

Do not assume the three concepts must use the same underlying balance or calculation.

Assess current scoring implementation and recommend the cleanest structure.

# 15. Variable prop values

We may want different props to be worth different amounts.

Examples:

Standard Draft Day prop:
10 points

Featured prop:
20 points

Season-end prediction made on Draft Day:
30–50 points

Examples:

* picking the eventual league champion may be worth more
* picking the highest-scoring regular-season team may be worth more
* a routine weekly prop may be worth less

Assess whether point value or scoring weight should belong to the prop itself.

We want flexibility without hard-coding scoring rules into competition types.

Recommend the minimum schema change if needed.

Do not design a complicated wagering or confidence system.

# 28. Final architectural challenge

Answer these questions explicitly.

### Question 1

If we finish Draft Day and immediately start building Week 1, what Draft Day code, schema, membership records, scoring records, or relationships would we need to undo or fundamentally restructure?

Our target should be:

**Very little or none.**

If significant rework would be necessary, revise your architecture.

### Question 2

Can a member who joins on Draft Day participate in Week 1 without re-entering their identity or rejoining the league?

If not, revise the model.

### Question 3

Can a season-long prop created on Draft Day remain unresolved while Draft Day is finalized and a Draft Day winner is declared?

If not, revise the model.

### Question 4

Can the same architecture support fantasy basketball or fantasy baseball without changing core league/member/competition relationships?

If not, revise the model.

### Question 5

Can manual league setup later be replaced or supplemented by Sleeper/Yahoo/API import without changing the internal Fantasy League model?

If not, revise the model.

### Question 6

Can a weekly reward be added without introducing payment custody or rebuilding the competition model?

If not, revise the model.
