We are making a focused product-language and question-library upgrade to Swayger Fantasy Weekly:

# SWAYGER MOMENTS — NAMED WEEKLY QUESTION STANDARD + LIBRARY EXPANSION

This is NOT a new major phase.

We want every Weekly Fantasy question to feel like a memorable Swayger moment rather than a generic stat question.

The product standard is:

DISPLAY TITLE
Short, memorable, personality-driven Swayger Moment name.

DISPLAY PROMPT
Clear plain-English question explaining exactly what users are predicting.

SETTLEMENT DEFINITION
Objective internal definition that makes commissioner settlement unambiguous.

SHARE-READY FRAMING
The question should be phrased in a way that can later support a “Share My Pick / Call Your Shot” experience without requiring the library to be redesigned.

Example:

BAD BEAT OF THE WEEK

Who will be the highest-scoring team that still loses?

Settlement definition:
Highest fantasy score among losing teams.

Future share framing:
“I’ve got {answer} for Bad Beat of the Week.”

The personality comes from the title.
The clarity comes from the prompt.
The settlement remains objective.
The Moment should also be worth talking about.

Internal design principles:

FUN TO PREDICT.
OBVIOUS TO SETTLE.
WORTH TALKING ABOUT.

Call Your Shot

1. stable `template_prop_id`

6. optional share-friendly phrasing potential

“I’ve got {answer} for Bad Beat of the Week.”

BASEMENT WATCH

Prompt:

“I’ve got {answer} on Basement Watch this week.”

WHO GETS AWAY WITH ONE?

Prompt:
Who will be the lowest-scoring team that still wins?

Future share phrase:
“I’ve got {answer} getting away with one this week.”

- Share My Pick UI
