We are implementing the next focused polish phase of Swayger Fantasy Weekly.

# SWAYGER RUN V1B — MY LOCK + SHARE FLOW + NAVIGATION POLISH

Context:

Swayger Run V1A is already built and manually QA’d.

What is working well:
- One-card focus mode
- Named Swayger Moments
- Dynamic progress
- Server-confirmed acknowledgements
- Resume behavior
- Completion summary
- Existing Call Your Shot / Share Pick architecture
- Existing feature-flag rollback

Manual QA surfaced a few UX improvements we now want to make.

This task should refine the experience without changing:
- scoring
- settlement
- published props
- answer options
- Weekly Results
- Weekly Receipt
- League Picks rules
- participant identity architecture
- existing share privacy

Product vocabulary going forward:

SWAYGER MOMENT
= the branded prediction question

PICK
= the action the participant makes

MY LOCK
= the ONE prediction the participant is most confident in

CALL YOUR SHOT
= choosing My Lock

SHARE PICK
= optionally sharing any individual confirmed pick

SHARE MY LOCK
= optionally sharing the selected My Lock

Core principle:

CALL YOUR SHOT SHOULD NOT MEAN “OPEN THE SHARE SHEET.”

Calling your shot should first create a meaningful state:
“This is the pick I’m standing on.”

Sharing comes after that and remains optional.

3. EDIT FLOW — ADD DONE / BACK TO SUMMARY
==================================================

Manual QA issue:

After someone has completed all picks, if they edit one earlier pick, they currently have to continue navigating through later picks to get back to completion.

Fix this.

When a participant has already completed the Weekly Swayger and is editing a confirmed pick:

show:

- Done
- Next Pick

“Done” should return immediately to:

YOUR SWAYGER IS IN

completion summary.

Behavior:

- edited answer must save successfully first
- show CALL UPDATED
- Done returns to summary
- no full completion celebration
- no final completion haptic
- no forced traversal through all remaining picks

For incomplete participants:
do not show Done-to-summary because completion summary should not exist yet.

==================================================
5. MY LOCK — PRODUCT MODEL
==================================================

Add persisted My Lock.

Definition:

My Lock = the ONE confirmed Weekly prediction that the participant is most confident in.

My Lock has NO scoring effect.

It must NOT:
- add SP
- multiply points
- affect settlement
- affect standings
- affect correctness
- affect Weekly Results scoring

It is purely:
- confidence
- identity
- expression
- sharing

==================================================
6. MY LOCK DATA MODEL
==================================================

Use the smallest safe additive persistence model.

Preferred conceptual table:

`fantasy_weekly_my_locks`

Fields conceptually:

- id
- room_id
- participant_id
- prop_id
- created_at
- updated_at

Uniqueness:

UNIQUE (room_id, participant_id)

Do NOT store:
- answer label
- team name
- share copy
- auth user ID directly if canonical participant identity already exists

Associate My Lock with:
- canonical gameday participant
- Weekly room
- selected prop

My Lock should resolve the current confirmed answer from the existing pick.

This means if the participant edits the answer for that Moment:
- My Lock remains attached to the Moment
- displayed/shared answer updates only after successful save

Server validation must confirm:
- room is Fantasy Weekly
- participant belongs to room
- prop belongs to room
- participant has a confirmed pick for prop
- room is still in a lifecycle state where My Lock can be selected/changed

Do not trust client-only validation.

If schema is required:
prepare the additive migration and STOP with:

SQL APPLICATION REQUIRED

before implementing dependent UI/API work.

==================================================
7. MY LOCK LIFECYCLE
==================================================

Before Week lock:

Participant may:
- select My Lock
- change My Lock
- skip My Lock
- return later and choose one

After Week lock:

My Lock becomes read-only.

After finalization/archive:

My Lock remains preserved for historical display if the Weekly summary is viewable.

Do not allow mutation after lock/finalization.

8. CALL YOUR SHOT — REDEFINE THE FLOW
==================================================

Current problem:

Call Your Shot currently leads directly into choosing a pick for sharing, which makes it feel like calling your shot = sharing.

Change the meaning.

New flow:

YOUR SWAYGER IS IN

[summary]

CALL YOUR SHOT

Which pick are you most confident in?

[Choose My Lock]

After choosing:

🔒 MY LOCK

BASEMENT WATCH
All Eyez On Me

This is the one you’re standing on.

[Share My Lock]
[Done]

Important:

Choosing My Lock completes Call Your Shot.

Sharing is optional.

If user chooses My Lock and then decides not to share:
they are still done.

Returning to completion summary should clearly show the selected My Lock.

==================================================
11. SHARE MY LOCK
==================================================

After My Lock exists:

Offer:

Share My Lock

Reuse existing Call Your Shot share architecture.

Share copy should add a My Lock label only when the shared pick is actually the current My Lock.

Example:

🔒 MY LOCK

BASEMENT WATCH

Darius has All Eyez On Me finishing with the fewest fantasy points this week.

Who you got?
<Weekly link>

If participant shares another confirmed pick using Share Pick:
use normal non-lock share copy.

Do not label every shared pick as My Lock.

==================================================
12. INDIVIDUAL SHARE PICK REMAINS INDEPENDENT
==================================================

A participant can:

- share any confirmed pick
- select My Lock
- share My Lock

Do not require My Lock to use Share Pick.

Do not require sharing to select My Lock.
