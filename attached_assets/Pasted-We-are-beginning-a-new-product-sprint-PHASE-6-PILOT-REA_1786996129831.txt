We are beginning a new product sprint:

# PHASE 6 — PILOT READINESS
## Commissioner Setup + Social Pick Reveal

Swayger Fantasy now has a functioning end-to-end season loop:

- Fantasy league setup
- persistent league/member/team identities
- Draft Day Swayger
- weekly Swaygers
- repeatable Week N → Week N+1 workflow
- guest/auth joining
- guest → account upgrade
- commissioner-assisted account recovery
- open roster lifecycle
- picks
- locking
- result settlement/correction
- finalization
- season standings
- weekly sharing/reminders
- commissioner NEXT UP workflow
- default weekly questions
- Use Last Week's Questions

Phase 5.3 is complete.

We are intentionally NOT beginning another major feature expansion.

The goal now is:

# MAKE SWAYGER FANTASY READY TO PUT IN FRONT OF A REAL 10–15 PERSON FANTASY LEAGUE

This sprint should remove the most obvious friction and make the social payoff stronger.

The central product question is:

Can one commissioner set up a real league, invite 10–15 people, run Draft Day and weekly Swaygers, and have the experience feel polished enough that we can learn from behavior rather than from preventable UX problems?

19. AREA C — POST-LOCK PICK REVEAL
==================================================

This is more than polish.

This is a Swayger product-value feature.

Core principle:

# PICKS ARE PRIVATE WHILE OPEN.
# PICKS BECOME SOCIAL ONCE LOCKED.

While picks are open:

NO player can see:

- vote distribution
- who picked what
- percentages
- other members' selections

Otherwise later players could be influenced.
