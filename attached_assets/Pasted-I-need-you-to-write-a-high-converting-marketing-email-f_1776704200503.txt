I need you to write a high-converting marketing email for Swayger, a social sports challenge app built around making picks, challenging friends, and sharing receipts/results.

This email is NOT a generic product announcement.
It is a direct challenge email from Swayger HQ to each user.

Important campaign mechanic:
- A unique Swayger challenge has ALREADY been created by Swayger HQ for every individual user in our database.
- The email goes out to everyone in the database.
- The email should clearly say that Swayger HQ is challenging the recipient to an NBA Picks Swayger.
- The main CTA in the email is: “Accept the Challenge”
- That CTA should conceptually take the user to their pre-created Swayger acceptance page from Swayger HQ.
- Once the user accepts the challenge, they make their nightly NBA playoff picks.
- Immediately after accepting and/or making picks, they should be encouraged to create another Swayger and challenge someone else.
- Later, they should also be encouraged to share their result/receipt.

Core user flow:
1. Swayger HQ challenges you
2. You click “Accept the Challenge”
3. You accept your pre-created NBA Picks Swayger
4. You make your picks
5. You challenge someone else
6. You share the result

Campaign objective:
Increase the percentage of users who accept their pre-created HQ challenge and then create at least one additional Swayger immediately after.

Audience:
- About 40 users total
- All are marginally invested in Swayger
- We do NOT want segmented email versions
- We want one unified email that works for everyone

Positioning:
This should feel like:
- a direct challenge
- an easy way to get involved
- an easy way back in
- an easy way to connect with someone else
- a simple nightly action during the NBA Playoffs

Brand and voice guidance:
- Brand: Swayger
- Sender persona: Swayger HQ
- Tone: confident, competitive, social, sharp
- Should feel like a smart sports-obsessed friend in a group chat
- Avoid corporate language
- Avoid sounding like gambling or sportsbook copy
- Emphasize proving your take, challenging friends, receipts, and bragging rights
- Keep it concise and action-oriented
