1. GOAL
==================================================

Make roster-target questions feel clean and usable for real 10–16 person fantasy leagues.

Current problem:

The play screen renders every answer option inline.

A 15-member league with 5 roster-based questions can create 75+ visible answer buttons.

This creates:

- excessive scrolling
- visual clutter
- poor mobile usability
- harder pick review
- higher chance of mis-taps

The goal is:

# KEEP QUESTION CARDS COMPACT WHILE MAKING 10–16 TEAM SELECTION FAST AND OBVIOUS.
