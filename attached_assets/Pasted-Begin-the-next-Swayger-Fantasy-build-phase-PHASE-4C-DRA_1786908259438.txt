2. CORE SCORING RULE
====================

Fantasy Draft Day scoring must use:

SUM(point_value for each correct competition-scope pick)

NOT:

correct picks × 10

Each published gameday_prop already has:

point_value

Use the published prop snapshot as authoritative.

For legacy non-Fantasy Game Day behavior:

preserve current 10-point behavior where appropriate.

Do not accidentally change classic Game Day scoring unless the generalized point_value behavior is explicitly backward-compatible.

Audit every existing location that currently calculates:

correct_count * 10

or equivalent.

Return every location found and how it was handled.

==================================================
3. TWO SCORING SCOPES MUST REMAIN SEPARATE
==========================================

Fantasy Draft Day contains:

A. `scoring_scope='competition'`

Examples:

* Who drafts the first QB?
* Who drafts the most RBs?
* Who drafts three QBs?

These:

* resolve during/end of Draft Day
* count toward Draft Day score
* determine Draft Day winner

B. `scoring_scope='season'`

Season Receipts.

Examples:

* Who finishes first?
* Who finishes last?
* Who scores the most points this season?

These:

* were picked on Draft Day
* are locked with Draft Day
* remain pending
* are NOT required to finalize Draft Day
* contribute ZERO to Draft Day leaderboard
* do NOT affect Draft Day winner
* will be settled later in a future phase

This separation is non-negotiable.
