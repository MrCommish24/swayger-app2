# THE MARK

## Icon: Locked Positions
Two sealed squares flanking a forward slash. The squares represent two locked positions — your take and theirs. The slash is the decisive line drawn between them. The three elements read as a single compound mark at any size.

**SVG coordinates (80×80 viewBox):**
```
Left square:  <rect x="9" y="34" width="12" height="12" rx="2"/>
Slash:        <polygon points="25,55 36,60 55,25 44,20"/>
Right square: <rect x="59" y="34" width="12" height="12" rx="2"/>
```

## Logo System

| Variant | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Stacked (mark above wordmark + tagline) | Primary — hero graphics, app splash, campaign launches |
| Horizontal (mark left of wordmark) | Secondary — headers, nav bars, email footers |
| Mark only | App icon, social avatar, favicon, emboss/embroidery |
| Wordmark only | Text-only contexts where the mark cannot render |

## Tagline
**Primary:** "Lock it in. Stand on it."
**Color:** Always Swayger Indigo (#4361EE) — never white, never gray
**Typeset:** DM Sans 500, ALL CAPS, letter-spacing 0.22em

**Alternative taglines:**
- "Make your call. Prove it."
- "Make your wager. Draw the line." (campaign/season use)

## Surface Colors

## Semantic Colors (use only for their meaning — never decoratively)

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Proof Green** | `#10B981` | Win states, "CORRECT ✓", receipt confirmed, positive outcomes |
| **Heat Red** | `#EF4444` | Loss states, "WRONG ✗", urgency, challenge callouts |
| **Championship Gold** | `#F59E0B` | Finals content and prize announcements ONLY — 2–3 uses per season max |

## Color Rules
- **Dark-first:** Every primary brand application starts on Void Black. No exceptions.
- **One accent per graphic:** Use Swayger Indigo OR Proof Green OR Heat Red per post — never two semantic colors together.
- **Gold is earned:** Championship Gold should only appear when a championship or prize is the literal subject. It means something. Overusing it destroys that.
- **White mark on color:** The mark always renders in Signal White on any colored background (Indigo, Green, Red, Gold).

# TYPOGRAPHY

## Primary Font: Barlow Condensed
**Google Fonts:** fonts.google.com/specimen/Barlow+Condensed
**Weights used:** 700 (SemiBold labels), 800 (Bold — primary weight)
**Usage:** Headlines, display text, wordmark, post overlays, social graphics
**Always ALL CAPS.** Never set Barlow Condensed in sentence case.
**Tracking:** 0 to -0.02em for headlines. Positive tracking (+0.05 to +0.12em) for the wordmark lockup only.

**Import:**
```
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Barlow+Condensed:wght@700;800&display=swap');
```

**Google Fonts:** fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans
**Weights used:** 400 (Regular body), 500 (Medium labels/tagline)

**Import:**

```
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans:wght@400;500&display=swap');
```

# LOGO USAGE RULES

## Dos
- Dark background always as the default application
- Minimum clear space = height of the mark's squares on all 4 sides
- Use mark-only at avatar/icon sizes (below 80px)
- White mark on any solid colored background
- Tagline always in Swayger Indigo when shown with the primary lockup

## Don'ts
- Never place the logo on a busy photographic background without a dark overlay
- Never stretch, rotate, skew, or apply effects to the mark
- Never use the wordmark in any color other than Signal White (dark bg) or Void Black (light bg)
- Never use the Zinc surface color (#27272A) as a background for the logo — too low contrast
- Never add a drop shadow or glow to the mark
- Never place the mark without the full lockup in marketing contexts (only at small sizes for avatars/icons)

## Minimum Sizes
- Mark + wordmark + tagline (stacked): 140px wide minimum
- Mark + wordmark (horizontal): 120px wide minimum
- Mark only: 20px wide minimum (below 16px, use wordmark text only)

## Background Rules
- **Void Black (#09090B):** Primary. Mark in Signal White. Always works.
- **Swayger Indigo (#4361EE):** Approved secondary. Mark in Signal White.
- **Signal White (#FAFAFA):** Approved light bg. Mark in Void Black. Tagline in Swayger Indigo.
- **Zinc (#27272A):** Approved for small mark-only contexts (avatar borders). Mark in Signal White.
- **Avoid midtones:** Do not place on grey, blue-grey, or any background where neither the white nor dark mark has sufficient contrast.

# POST TEMPLATE SYSTEM

## Template 1: Challenge / Hype Post
**Use:** Campaign launches, round openers, sign-up pushes, any "get in here" moment

**Layout:**
- Background: Void Black (#09090B)
- Thin vertical Swayger Indigo rule on left edge (8px wide, full height)
- Headline: Barlow Condensed 800, white, massive (64–88px depending on word count), ALL CAPS, left-aligned or centered
- Supporting copy: DM Sans 400, Slate (#A8A8B3), 14px
- Bottom-right: Swayger mark (32px) + URL in Slate (11px)

**Headline examples:**
- "WHO YOU GOT?"
- "SOMEBODY IN YOUR GROUP CHAT IS ABOUT TO BE WRONG."
- "LOCKED IN."
- "STOP JUST TALKING."

## Template 2: Pick Card / Take Reveal
**Use:** When Swayger posts its own picks, or for pick reveal content

**Layout:**
- Background: Void Black
- Top label: "THE CALL:" in Swayger Indigo (#4361EE), DM Sans 500, 11px, letter-spacing 0.14em, ALL CAPS
- Pick/Team name: Barlow Condensed 800, white, 72–88px
- Details line: "IN [X] GAMES · [ROUND NAME]" in DM Sans 500, Slate, 13px
- Confidence indicator (optional): "HIGH CONVICTION" / "LOCK" / "LEAN" in Indigo pill badge
- Bottom: "On record via Swayger" + mark + URL

## Template 3: Receipt / Result Post
**Use:** Series clinches, round results, "I called it" moments

**Layout:**
- Background: Void Black (#09090B)
- Center: Warm cream card (#FEF9EF), 76% of post width, rounded corners (8px)
- Card header: "SWAYGER" in Space Mono or Courier New, 12px, Void Black
- Dividing line below header
- Card body (monospace):
  ```
  CONTRACT #[XXXXX]
  Series: [Team A] vs. [Team B]
  Pick placed: [Team A] to win
  Round: [Round Name]
  ─────────────────────────
  RESULT: [Team A] wins
  ```
- Outcome stamp: "CORRECT ✓" in Proof Green (Barlow Condensed 800, 28px) OR "WRONG ✗" in Heat Red — rotated 8° as a stamp overlay
- Card footer: "Receipt confirmed · swayger-app.replit.app"

## Template 4: Prize / Winner Announcement
**Use:** Round transitions, prize distribution, winner callouts

**Layout:**
- Background: Void Black
- Dollar amount: "$50" (or applicable) in Barlow Condensed 800, Championship Gold (#F59E0B), 96px
- Context: "NBA FINALS PRIZE POOL" in DM Sans 500, Slate, 11px, tracking 0.14em
- Sub-copy: DM Sans 400, white, 14px
- Mark + URL at bottom

# BRAND DON'TS

These are non-negotiable. Every team member and every vendor should know them.

1. **No gradients on the mark.** The mark is always flat, one color.
2. **No glow or drop shadow on the mark.** Background atmosphere is ok. Effects on the mark itself are not.
3. **No casino/sportsbook language.** "Odds," "lines," "bet" — all avoided. Swayger is about picks and accountability, not gambling mechanics.
4. **Tagline always in Swayger Indigo.** Never white. Never gray.
5. **Championship Gold maximum 3 posts per playoff season.** If it shows up on every post, it loses meaning.
6. **No font substitutions.** Barlow Condensed 800 and DM Sans 400/500. No Impact, no Arial Black.
7. **No all-caps on DM Sans.** Ever.
8. **No emojis in designed graphics.** Captions: fine. Text overlays: never.
