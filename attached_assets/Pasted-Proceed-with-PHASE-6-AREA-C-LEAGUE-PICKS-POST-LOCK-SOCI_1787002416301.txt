2. PRIVACY RULE — NON-NEGOTIABLE
==================================================

While picks are OPEN:

A member must NOT be able to see:

- another member's answer
- answer counts
- answer percentages
- picker names
- partial distribution
- "most popular" answer

This applies to:

- regular authenticated member
- guest member

- co-commissioner

may continue to exist.
