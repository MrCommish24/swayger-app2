We are beginning the next major Swayger Fantasy phase.

# PHASE 5 — SWAYGER FANTASY SEASON

Phase 4C Draft Day is complete enough to move forward.

Product naming:

- Swayger = umbrella product
- Swayger 1v1 = head-to-head Swaygers
- Swayger Game Day = live event / Game Day Rooms
- Swayger Fantasy = fantasy-league engagement layer
  - Draft Day
  - Fantasy Season

Customer-facing recurring competitions should be called:

Week 1 Swayger
Week 2 Swayger
Week 3 Swayger
etc.

The goal of this phase is NOT to build the whole season.

The goal is:

Draft Day
→ Week 1 Swayger
→ Week 1 Winner
→ cumulative Fantasy Season Standings

This is our first retention-loop test.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Swayger Fantasy does NOT replace ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, etc.

Those products handle:
- rosters
- lineups
- waivers
- fantasy scoring
- league matchup administration

Swayger is the engagement/prediction layer on top of the existing fantasy league.

The recurring question is:

“Who knows this league best?”

Draft Day gets the league into Swayger.

Fantasy Season gives them another competition every week.

21. COMPETITIONS PLAYED
==================================================

Define this deliberately.

A competition counts as “played” when:

member submitted at least one competition-scope pick.

Do NOT count someone merely because:
- they are a league member
- they opened the play screen
- a gameday_participant row exists

We already discovered that participant-row existence alone can create phantom zero-score entries.

Season standings should be based on actual participation.
