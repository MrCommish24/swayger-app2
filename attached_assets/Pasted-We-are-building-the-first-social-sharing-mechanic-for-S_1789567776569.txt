We are building the first social-sharing mechanic for Swayger Fantasy Weekly.

# CALL YOUR SHOT — SHARE MY PICK V1

Context:

Swayger Fantasy Weekly now has 12 named Swayger Moments such as:

- SCOREBOARD KING
- BASEMENT WATCH
- BIGGEST BLOWOUT
- ESCAPE ARTIST
- STAR POWER
- THE 150 CLUB
- PHOTO FINISH
- BAD BEAT OF THE WEEK
- WHO GETS AWAY WITH ONE?
- FRAUD WATCH
- HEARTBREAKER
- STATEMENT WIN

These are derived from stable `template_prop_id` values through the existing Swayger Moment registry.

The next product step is to let a participant voluntarily share one of their weekly predictions into an existing league group chat.

The product idea:

A participant makes a prediction.
They choose to publicly “Call Their Shot.”
The shared message names the Swayger Moment, their prediction, and invites league mates to make their own picks.

This is a distribution and engagement mechanic.

It is NOT a public social feed.

Core product loop:

PICK
→ CALL YOUR SHOT
→ SHARE
→ LEAGUE REACTS
→ LOCK
→ LEAGUE PICKS REVEAL
→ SETTLEMENT
→ RECEIPT

Product principles:

VOLUNTARY BEFORE-LOCK DISCLOSURE.
ONE PICK CAN BE SHARED WITHOUT REVEALING THE REST.
PERSONALITY FROM THE SWAYGER MOMENT.
DEEP LINK BACK INTO THE WEEK.
NO PUBLIC PRE-LOCK LEAGUE-PICK EXPOSURE.

3. PRIVACY MODEL
==================================================

This is critical.

Before Weekly lock:

- participant picks remain private by default
- League Picks aggregate behavior remains unchanged
- no participant’s picks may be queried publicly
- no public API should expose saved picks
- no pick becomes generally visible merely because sharing exists

A participant may intentionally disclose exactly ONE selected pick by invoking Share My Pick.

The share package may contain only information the participant deliberately chose to share:

- league display name if appropriate
- Week number
- participant safe display name
- Swayger Moment display title
- their selected answer rendered safely
- plain-English share framing
- current Week participation URL
- generic Swayger branding

Sharing one pick must NOT reveal:

- other picks
- League Picks distribution
- other participants’ picks
- participant IDs
- member IDs
- user IDs
- claim IDs
- guest tokens
- recovery tokens
- emails
- phone numbers
- auth/session credentials

4. PARTICIPANT UX — PER-QUESTION ACTION
==================================================

After a participant has selected an answer for a Weekly question, expose a subtle action on that question card:
